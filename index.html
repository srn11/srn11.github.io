We are a small practice working on personal compute infrastructure, AI agent systems, and the trust frameworks that connect them. We build the apparatus that lets one person, augmented by accountable agents, do serious work without depending on platforms whose interests are not aligned with theirs.

Our practice is technical and patient: working systems running today, evolving toward a future in which sovereign personal computing is not a niche but a viable alternative to the rented apparatus most people currently inhabit.
